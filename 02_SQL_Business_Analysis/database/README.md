# SQL Business Analysis Project

## Project Overview
This project analyzes an online store's sales data using SQL and SQLite to identify business trends, evaluate product and regional performance, and understand customer purchasing behavior.

The analysis focuses on sales performance, profitability, customer segmentation, discount effectiveness, and quarterly trends.

## Dataset
- **Records:** 1,000 orders
- **Columns:** 18
- **Period:** January–December 2025
- **Format:** CSV
- **Database:** SQLite

## Tools & Technologies
- SQL
- SQLite
- DB Browser for SQLite
- GitHub

## Analysis Areas
1. Overall Business Performance and KPIs
2. Regional Sales Performance
3. Category and Product Analysis
4. Monthly and Quarterly Sales Trends
5. Product Profitability and Ranking
6. Customer Value and Segmentation
7. Repeat Customer Analysis
8. Discount Performance
9. Product Pareto and ABC Analysis

## Key Business Findings
- Total revenue reached **$149,501.95**, with gross profit of **$67,832.95**.
- Overall gross profit margin was **45.37%**.
- The **Home** category generated the largest share of revenue, approximately **60.8%**.
- The **North** region generated the highest revenue and gross profit.
- **Office Chair** was the leading product by revenue, contributing approximately **48.3%** of total sales revenue, but had a below-average profit margin.
- **Pen Set** achieved the highest product profit margin at **70.45%**.
